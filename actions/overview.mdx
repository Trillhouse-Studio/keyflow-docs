Actions enable human-in-the-loop workflows where your automation can pause and request human input, approval, or feedback before continuing. This is essential for building reliable business systems that combine AI efficiency with human judgment.

## What Are Actions?

When your workflows need human review, they create actions - requests for human input that appear in your action center. The workflow pauses until someone provides the requested feedback, then continues based on that input.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/actions/action-center-overview.png" alt="Action center showing pending human reviews" />
</Frame>

Actions help you build systems where AI handles the heavy lifting but humans make the final decisions on important matters.

## Why Human-in-the-Loop Matters

AI is powerful, but human oversight ensures quality and handles edge cases that automated systems might miss. Human-in-the-loop workflows let you:

- **Increase output quality** with human review and approval
- **Handle complex decisions** that require human judgment
- **Build trustworthy systems** for important business processes
- **Maintain control** over automated workflows

This approach is particularly valuable for customer-facing communications, financial decisions, or any process where accuracy is critical.

## When to Use Actions

Consider adding human review points when:

- **Quality matters more than speed** - important communications or decisions
- **AI confidence is low** - complex or ambiguous situations
- **Business risk is high** - processes with significant impact
- **Learning opportunities exist** - building better prompts from human feedback
