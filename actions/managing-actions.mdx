The action center is where you view and complete all human review requests from your workflows. When workflows pause for human input, actions appear here waiting for your feedback.

## Action Center Overview

Access the action center from the main sidebar to see all actions across your workspace. This gives you a centralized view of everything waiting for human review.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/actions/action-center-dashboard.png" alt="Action center showing pending and completed actions" />
</Frame>

The action center shows both pending actions that need your attention and completed actions for reference.

## Viewing Pending Actions

Pending actions are requests waiting for human input. Each pending action shows:

- **Workflow name** that created the action
- **Action type** (Ask Human block or AI review)
- **Creation time** so you know how long it's been waiting
- **Brief description** of what feedback is needed

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/actions/pending-actions-list.png" alt="List of pending actions awaiting review" />
</Frame>

Click on any pending action to see the full details and provide your feedback.

## Taking Action

When you open a pending action, you'll see the complete context and feedback form. This includes:

- **Original request** with instructions for the reviewer
- **Context information** like AI outputs or supporting data
- **Feedback form** with the specific inputs needed
- **Workflow information** showing where this fits in the process

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/actions/action-details-form.png" alt="Action details page with feedback form" />
</Frame>

Provide your feedback through the form and submit to continue the workflow. The action moves to completed status and the workflow resumes with your input.

## Completed Actions

Completed actions show the history of human feedback in your workflows. You can review:

- **What feedback was requested** and provided
- **Who completed the action** and when
- **How the workflow continued** after receiving feedback
- **Final outcomes** of the workflow execution

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/actions/completed-actions-history.png" alt="History of completed actions" />
</Frame>

This history helps you understand how human input affects your workflows and identify patterns in the types of feedback needed.

## Action Notifications

Stay informed about actions that need your attention:

- **In-app notifications** when new actions are created
- **Email alerts** for time-sensitive reviews
- **Dashboard indicators** showing pending action counts

Configure notification preferences in your account settings to ensure you don't miss important review requests.

## Collaborative Review

When multiple team members have access to actions, you can:

- **Assign specific actions** to appropriate reviewers
- **Add comments** to actions for team communication
- **Track who completed** each review for accountability
- **Share context** about decisions made during review

This collaborative approach ensures the right people review the right actions and maintains visibility into the human decision-making process.

## Action Best Practices

### Respond Promptly

Workflows pause until actions are completed, so timely responses keep your automation running smoothly. Set up notifications and check the action center regularly.

### Provide Quality Feedback

Your feedback directly impacts workflow outcomes:

- Read instructions carefully before responding
- Consider the broader context of the workflow
- Provide clear, actionable feedback
- Document reasoning for complex decisions

### Monitor Action Patterns

Review completed actions periodically to identify:

- Common types of feedback requests
- Opportunities to improve AI prompts
- Steps that might be automated further
- Quality issues in workflow outputs

This analysis helps you continuously improve your human-in-the-loop workflows.
