Flows help you create intelligent systems that handle complex business processes automatically. Think of them as smart workflows that can read documents, make decisions, update systems, and even loop in humans when needed.

Unlike simple automation tools, flows combine AI with integrations to create systems that actually understand context and adapt to different situations. You build them once, then deploy them as apps, trigger them via APIs, or run them on schedules.

## Why This Matters

Most companies have the same problem - too much repetitive work and not enough people to do it. Flows solve this by turning manual processes into intelligent systems.

A few days spent building these systems saves hours of operational overhead every week. Teams accomplish more with smaller headcount while making work more exciting. Instead of doing the same manual tasks over and over, people spend time on strategy, creativity, and building even better systems. The repetitive work just happens automatically in the background.

Companies that embrace this approach transform their culture around automation. Work becomes more strategic, less reactive.
