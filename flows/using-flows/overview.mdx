You've built and tested your workflow - now it's time to put it to work. Instead of manually running flows every time you need them, you can deploy them in different ways that fit seamlessly into your daily operations.

Whether you want a user-friendly app interface, automated triggers, or integration with external tools, Keyflow gives you multiple ways to turn your one-time workflow into a system that runs continuously.

## App View - Internal Tools

Turn your flow into a user-friendly internal app that anyone on your team can use. The app view creates a clean interface where users input data and get results without seeing the complexity underneath.

<Frame>
 <img src="/images/deploying/app-view-interface.png" alt="Flow deployed as internal app with input forms" />
</Frame>

To create an app, define your flow's inputs and outputs clearly. Keyflow automatically generates a form for inputs and displays outputs in an organized way.

**Example**: Transform your document analysis flow into an app where team members upload PDFs and get structured summaries, key insights, and action items - no technical knowledge required.

## API Integration

Remotely trigger your workflows using APIs to integrate Keyflow into existing systems. This lets you embed your intelligent processes anywhere in your tech stack.

<Frame>
 <img src="/images/deploying/api-integration.png" alt="API endpoint configuration and usage" />
</Frame>

Every flow can become an API endpoint that accepts inputs and returns outputs. Perfect for connecting to websites, mobile apps, or other internal tools.

**Example**: Connect your lead qualification flow to your website's contact form. When someone submits their information, the API automatically researches the company, scores the lead, and updates your CRM with enriched data.

## Scheduled Automation

Set workflows to run automatically on schedules - daily, weekly, or any custom timing you need. Perfect for recurring processes that should happen without manual intervention.

<Frame>
 <img src="/images/deploying/scheduled-workflows.png" alt="Schedule configuration interface" />
</Frame>

**Example**: Schedule your competitor analysis flow to run every Monday morning, automatically scraping competitor websites, analyzing their content changes, and sending your team a weekly intelligence report.

## MCP Toolkits

Turn your workflows into tools that work inside Claude, Cursor, Claude Code, or any app with MCP support. This brings your custom processes directly into the AI tools you already use.

<Frame>
 <img src="/images/deploying/mcp-toolkit.png" alt="Workflow converted to MCP toolkit in Claude" />
</Frame>

MCP toolkits are incredibly powerful for AI power users who want to extend their favorite apps with custom intelligence.

**Example**: Convert your market research flow into an MCP tool. Now when you're chatting with Claude about business strategy, you can instantly trigger deep market analysis and competitive intelligence gathering without leaving the conversation.

## Agent Swarm

Process large volumes of data by running your workflow across multiple inputs simultaneously. Upload a CSV or create a table, and watch your workflow process hundreds or thousands of items in parallel.

<Frame>
 <img src="/images/deploying/agent-swarm.png" alt="Bulk processing interface with progress tracking" />
</Frame>

This is perfect when you need to apply the same intelligent process to many documents, leads, or data points at once.

**Example**: Upload a list of 500 potential customers and run your lead qualification flow on all of them simultaneously. Get back enriched data, qualification scores, and personalized outreach suggestions for your entire prospect list in minutes instead of weeks.
