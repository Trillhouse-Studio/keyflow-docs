## What Are MCP Toolkits?

MCP Toolkits let you bundle your workflows into tool servers that work with apps like Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, and Windsurf. Instead of building custom integrations or writing code, you simply select your workflows and get a URL that connects your automation to these popular development and AI tools.

  <img src="/images/toolkits/toolkit-overview.png" alt="MCP toolkit connecting workflows to external apps" />
</Frame>

This means you can extend the functionality of your daily driver apps without writing a single line of code - just build workflows in Keyflow and make them available as tools.

## Why This Matters

When you're working in Claude, Cursor, or other MCP-compatible apps, you often need custom functionality that doesn't exist out of the box. Instead of switching between tools or building complex integrations, MCP Toolkits let you bring your Keyflow automation directly into these apps.

Your workflows become tools that AI models and development environments can use on your behalf, creating seamless experiences where your automation enhances your existing workflow.

## Popular MCP-Compatible Apps

**Claude**: Use your workflows as tools in conversations with Claude
**Claude Code**: Integrate automation into your coding workflow
**Cursor**: Bring custom functionality into your development environment  
**Windsurf**: Extend your IDE with workflow-powered tools

Each app connects to your toolkit through a simple URL, making your workflows instantly available as native tools.

## How It Works

Creating and using toolkits is straightforward:

1. **Select workflows** you want to include in your toolkit
2. **Create the toolkit** and get a connection URL
3. **Connect to your apps** by pasting the URL in their MCP settings
4. **Use your workflows as tools** within those applications

The toolkit handles all the technical complexity of exposing your workflows as standardized tools.
