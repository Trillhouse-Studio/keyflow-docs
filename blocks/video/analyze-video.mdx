---
title: Analyze Video
---

### Overview

Analyze Video uses Google's Gemini AI models to extract insights, descriptions, and transcripts from video files. Upload any video and ask questions about its content, objects, people, or events.

<Tip>
  Perfect for video content analysis, accessibility transcription, and automated
  video summarization.
</Tip>

---

### Inputs

<ParamField query="Video" type="File" required>
  Upload the video file you want to analyze. Supports common video formats like
  MP4.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="Prompt" type="String" required>
  Describe the analysis you want to perform on the video. Be specific about what
  information you need.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="Model" type="String" default="Gemini 2.5 Flash Preview">
  Choose the AI model for video analysis. Flash models are faster while Pro
  models provide more detailed analysis.
</ParamField>

<br></br>

<Accordion title="Advanced Settings">
  <ParamField query="Cache Response" type="Boolean" default="true">
    Enable caching to store the model response for future use, improving performance for repeated analyses.
  </ParamField>

  <ParamField query="Account" type="String">
    Select the Google Gemini API credentials to use for authentication.
  </ParamField>
</Accordion>

### Outputs

<ParamField query="Transcript" type="String">
  The AI-generated analysis, description, or transcript based on your prompt and
  video content.
</ParamField>

---

### Best Practices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write Clear, Specific Prompts">
    Be detailed about what you want to extract from the video for better results.
    ```text ⚠️ Bad Example wrap
    Tell me about this video.
    ```
    ```text ✅ Good Example wrap
    Summarize the main events in this training video and list any safety equipment mentioned.
    ```
  </Step>

<Step title="Choose the Right Model">
  Use Flash models for quick summaries and Pro models for detailed analysis
  requiring deeper understanding.
</Step>

  <Step title="Optimize Video Files">
    Use standard formats like MP4 and ensure videos are reasonably sized for faster processing and better results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

### Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is my video analysis taking so long?">
    Large video files require more processing time. The system uploads your video to Google's servers and waits for it to be processed before analysis begins.
    
    <Tip>Try using shorter video clips or lower resolution files for faster results.</Tip>
  </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="What video formats are supported?">
        The block primarily supports MP4 format. Other common video formats may work
        but MP4 is recommended for best compatibility.
        <Info>
            The system automatically handles video upload and processing through
            Google's Gemini API.
        </Info>
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Why did my video analysis fail?">
    Common causes include unsupported file formats, corrupted videos, or API
    authentication issues.
    <Warning>
        Make sure your video file exists and is accessible before running the
        analysis.
    </Warning>
        Steps to resolve:
            1. Verify the video file plays correctly
            2. Check your Google Gemini API credentials
            3. Try with a different video format (MP4
        recommended)
    </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I get better analysis results?">
    The quality of results depends heavily on your prompt specificity and the chosen model.
    
    ```text Example for detailed analysis wrap
    Identify all people in the video, describe their actions, and note any objects they interact with. Include timestamps if possible.
    ```
    
    <Info>Pro models provide more detailed analysis but take longer than Flash models.</Info>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
