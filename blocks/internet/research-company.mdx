title: Research Company

### Overview

The Research Company block uses AI to generate a detailed report on a company's background, products, and competitors. Perfect for researching companies, identifying potential partnerships, or creating a marketing strategy.

<Tip>Transforms your data and instructions into professional, formatted reports ready for sharing.</Tip>

---

### Inputs

<ParamField query="Company Info" type="Text" required>
  Provide all information you have about the company - name, address, industry, etc.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="Smart Search" type="Toggle">
  Uses enrichment techniques to find information. If enrichment is missing information you requested, it will trigger a deep research to find that information.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="Deep Research Condition" type="Toggle">
    Condition that should trigger a deep research if its missing from enrichment results.
</ParamField>

<br></br>

<Accordion title="Advanced Settings">
  <ParamField query="Research Type" type="Dropdown">
    Manually pick between simple enrichment or deep research. Only visible when Smart Search is disabled.
  </ParamField>

  <ParamField query="Account" type="Select">
    Select the API credentials you want to use for the chosen model.
  </ParamField>
</Accordion>

### Outputs

<ParamField query="Report" type="Text">
  A comprehensive report on the company's background, products, and competitors, ready for sharing.
</ParamField>

---

### Best Practices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Provide Clear, Specific Information">
    Include all relevant information about the company in your prompt. This helps the AI understand the context and provide more accurate results.
    ```text ⚠️ Bad Example wrap
    Sales AI Agents
    ```
    ```text ✅ Good Example wrap
    Acme Inc., a sales AI Agents company, New York, USA
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use Smart Search for Enhanced Results">
    Enable smart search to find information that's missing from your enrichment results. If enrichment is missing information you requested, it will trigger a deep research to find that information.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Consider Deep Research for Missing Information">
    Disable smart search and enable deep research if you always want to do deep research..
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

### Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is my report missing specific information?">
    Ensure your prompt clearly specifies what information you want the AI to include in the report. The AI can only work with information you provide or reference.
    
    ```text Example Solution wrap
    Instead of: "Generate a report on Acme Inc."
    Try: "Generate a report on Acme Inc., a sales AI Agents company, New York, USA"
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I improve report formatting and structure?">
    <Tip>Be explicit about the desired format and sections in your prompt</Tip>
    1. Specify the report sections you want (executive summary, detailed analysis, recommendations)
    2. Request specific formatting (tables, bullet points, numbered lists)
    3. Include examples of similar reports you've liked
    <Warning>Very long or complex prompts may affect processing time</Warning>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
