title: Speech to Text

### Overview

The Speech to Text block converts audio files into written text using Google Cloud Speech-to-Text technology. Upload audio recordings in various formats and get accurate transcriptions with support for multiple languages.

<Tip>Convert audio recordings to text with high accuracy across multiple languages and audio formats.</Tip>

---

### Inputs

<ParamField query="Media File" type="file" required>
  The audio file you want to convert into text. Supports MP3, WAV, and other common audio formats.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="Language" type="string" default="en-US">
  The language of the audio for accurate transcription. Choose from supported languages like English, Spanish, Chinese, and more.
</ParamField>

### Outputs

<ParamField query="Transcription" type="string">
  The written text extracted from the audio file, formatted as a readable transcript.
</ParamField>

---

### Best Practices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use High-Quality Audio">
    Ensure clear audio with minimal background noise for better transcription accuracy.
    
    ```text ⚠️ Bad Example wrap
    Recording with heavy background music, multiple speakers talking over each other, poor microphone quality
    ```
    
    ```text ✅ Good Example wrap
    Clear single-speaker recording, quiet environment, good microphone quality, normal speaking pace
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the Correct Language">
    Choose the exact language variant that matches your audio for optimal results.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Optimize Audio Format">
    Use supported audio formats and ensure proper encoding for best results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is my audio file not being processed?">
    Check that your audio file is in a supported format and not corrupted.
    
    Supported formats:
    - MP3
    - WAV
    - FLAC
    - M4A
    - WebM
    
    <Warning>Very large audio files may take longer to process or exceed size limits</Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The transcription is inaccurate or has many errors">
    <Tip>Audio quality and clarity significantly impact transcription accuracy</Tip>
    
    Try these improvements:
    1. Use audio with less background noise
    2. Ensure speakers are speaking clearly and at normal pace
    3. Verify you've selected the correct language
    4. Use higher quality audio recordings (better microphone, less compression)
    5. Split very long audio files into shorter segments
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Some words are missing or incorrect">
    This is common with technical terms, names, or unclear speech.
    
    <Info>The service works best with clear, conversational speech</Info>
    
    Solutions:
    1. Ensure speakers pronounce words clearly
    2. Avoid heavy accents or very fast speech
    3. Consider manual review for critical transcriptions
    4. Use audio with consistent volume levels
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Processing takes a very long time">
    <Warning>Long audio files require more processing time</Warning>
    
    Factors affecting processing time:
    1. File length - longer files take more time
    2. Audio quality - complex audio requires more processing
    3. Server load - processing may be queued during high demand
    
    For faster results:
    - Break long recordings into shorter segments
    - Use simpler audio (single speaker, clear speech)
    - Process during off-peak hours
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Language not detected correctly">
    Make sure you've selected the exact language variant from the dropdown.
    
    Available languages include:
    - English (en-US)
    - Spanish (es-ES)
    - Chinese (zh-CN)
    - Hindi (hi-IN)
    - Arabic (ar-SA)
    - Portuguese (pt-BR)
    - French (fr-FR)
    - Russian (ru-RU)
    - Japanese (ja-JP)
    - German (de-DE)
    - Bengali (bn-IN)
    - Urdu (ur-PK)
    - Korean (ko-KR)
    - Vietnamese (vi-VN)
    - Gujarati (gu-IN)
    
    <Info>Using the wrong language setting will significantly reduce accuracy</Info>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
