title: Sentence Search
---

### Overview

Searches through large text documents using AI embeddings to find the most relevant sections based on your query. Automatically chunks text and uses semantic similarity to return the most relevant content.

<Tip>Perfect for finding specific information within large documents or texts using semantic similarity rather than keyword matching.</Tip>

---

### Inputs

<ParamField query="Text" type="String" required>
  The document or text content you want to search through.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="Query" type="String" required>
  Your search query - the system will find text sections most semantically similar to this query.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="No. of Results" type="Number" default="10">
  The number of relevant text chunks you want to retrieve from the search.
</ParamField>

<br></br>

<Accordion title="Advanced Settings">
  <ParamField query="Chunk Size" type="Number" default="1000">
    The number of tokens to include in each text chunk. Smaller chunks provide more precise results, larger chunks provide more context.
  </ParamField>
</Accordion>

### Outputs

<ParamField query="Relevant Chunks" type="String Array">
  Array of text chunks that are most semantically relevant to your query, ordered by similarity.
</ParamField>

---

### Best Practices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use Descriptive Queries">
    Write clear, descriptive queries that capture the meaning of what you're looking for rather than just keywords.
    ```text ⚠️ Bad Example wrap
    carbon
    ```
    ```text ✅ Good Example wrap
    Ways to reduce carbon footprint at domestic level
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Optimize Chunk Size for Your Content">
    Adjust chunk size based on your content type - use smaller chunks (500-800) for precise information retrieval, larger chunks (1000-1500) for context-rich results.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Balance Number of Results">
    Start with 5-10 results and adjust based on your needs. Too many results may include less relevant content, too few may miss important information.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

### Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why are my search results not relevant?">
    This could be due to unclear queries or inappropriate chunk sizes. Try making your query more specific and descriptive, or adjust the chunk size to better match your content structure.
    
    ```json Example Solution wrap
    {
      "query": "environmental sustainability practices in urban planning",
      "chunk_size": 800,
      "num_results": 5
    }
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get more precise results?">
    <Tip>Use smaller chunk sizes and more specific queries for precision</Tip>
    1. Reduce chunk size to 500-700 tokens
    2. Make your query more specific and detailed
    3. Reduce the number of results to focus on top matches
    <Warning>Very small chunks may lose important context</Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I'm not getting enough context in results?">
    Increase the chunk size to capture more context around relevant sections. Also consider increasing the number of results to get a broader view of relevant content.
    
    <Info>Larger chunk sizes provide more context but may reduce precision</Info>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
