### Overview

The Scorer block uses AI to assign a numerical score (1-100) to any content based on your custom criteria. Perfect for evaluating quality, ranking items, or making objective assessments with optional justifications.

<Tip>Get objective numerical scores for content evaluation with AI-powered analysis and optional reasoning.</Tip>

---

### Inputs

<ParamField query="Data" type="Text" required>
  The content you want to score - can be articles, emails, products, or any text you need evaluated.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="Criteria" type="Text" required>
  Define the scoring criteria such as "quality of writing", "conciseness", "relevance", or any custom evaluation standards.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="Include Justification" type="Toggle" >
  If checked, the model will provide detailed reasoning for why it assigned the specific score.
</ParamField>

<br></br>

<Accordion title="Advanced Settings">
  <ParamField query="Model" type="Select">
    The AI model to use for scoring. Choose based on your accuracy and cost requirements.
  </ParamField>

  <ParamField query="Cache Response" type="Toggle">
    If checked, identical scoring requests will be cached to save costs.
  </ParamField>

  <ParamField query="API Keys" type="Select">
    Select the API credentials you want to use for the chosen model.
  </ParamField>
</Accordion>

### Outputs

<ParamField query="Score" type="Text">
  A numerical score between 1 and 100 based on how well the content meets your specified criteria.
</ParamField>

<br></br>

<Accordion title="Additional Outputs">
  <ParamField query="Justification" type="Text">
    Detailed explanation for why the AI assigned this score (only when Include Justification is enabled).
  </ParamField>
</Accordion>

### Best Practices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define Clear, Specific Criteria">
    Be precise about what you're measuring to get consistent, meaningful scores.
    
    ```text ⚠️ Bad Example wrap
    Quality
    ```
    
    ```text ✅ Good Example wrap
    Quality of writing based on: clarity of arguments, grammar and spelling accuracy, logical flow, and engaging tone for a business audience
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use Consistent Scoring Standards">
    Maintain the same criteria format across similar evaluations for comparable results.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable Justification for Important Decisions">
    Use justifications when you need to understand or explain the scoring rationale to others.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Scores seem inconsistent or random">
    The scoring criteria may be too vague or subjective for consistent evaluation.
    
    <Tip>More specific criteria lead to more consistent scoring results</Tip>
    
    Try these improvements:
    1. Define specific measurable aspects in your criteria
    2. Include examples of what constitutes different score ranges
    3. Break complex criteria into specific sub-criteria
    4. Use objective measures where possible
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="All scores are coming back as very similar">
    Your criteria might be too broad or the content being scored is too similar.
    
    <Info>AI tends to give scores in the middle range when criteria are unclear</Info>
    
    Solutions:
    1. Make your criteria more discriminating
    2. Include specific benchmarks for high vs low scores
    3. Test with very different content to verify the scoring range
    4. Consider scoring different aspects separately
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Justifications don't match the scores">
    This can happen when criteria are ambiguous or conflicting.
    
    <Warning>Conflicting criteria can lead to inconsistent scoring logic</Warning>
    
    To fix this:
    1. Review and simplify your criteria
    2. Test with clear positive and negative examples
    3. Break complex criteria into separate scoring blocks
    4. Use more specific language about what constitutes each score range
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I score multiple criteria at once">
    You can score multiple aspects, but it's often better to score them separately for clarity.
    
    Options:
    1. Create separate Scorer blocks for each criterion
    2. Include multiple aspects in one comprehensive criteria description
    3. Use weighted criteria with specific percentages for each aspect
    
    <Info>Separate scoring often provides more actionable insights</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between score ranges">
    The 1-100 scale provides granular scoring, but you can interpret ranges based on your needs:
    
    Common interpretations:
    - 90-100: Excellent/Outstanding
    - 80-89: Good/Above Average
    - 70-79: Satisfactory/Average  
    - 60-69: Below Average/Needs Improvement
    - 1-59: Poor/Unacceptable
    
    <Info>You can define your own scale meanings in the criteria</Info>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
