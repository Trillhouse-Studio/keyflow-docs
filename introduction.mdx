Keyflow lets you rapidly and visually create intelligent systems to automate work.

Most business processes are just patterns: read documents, analyze data, make decisions, update systems. **Instead of doing this manually over and over, you build smart processes that handle it automatically.**

In Keyflow, you build workflows by connecting blocks that each do something specific: analyze text with AI, pull data from APIs, send emails, or request human input for complex decisions. These processes can include both AI and human intelligence in the loop—letting you automate what's routine while keeping humans involved where judgment matters.

<Tip>Spending one day to build a flow can save you thousands of hours of work throughout the year.</Tip>
